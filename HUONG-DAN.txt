TIKTOK LIVE BAR - HUONG DAN CHAY TREN MAY MOI
=============================================

1. Giai nen file TikTokLiveBar-....zip vao mot thu muc bat ky (vi du C:\TikTokLiveBar).
   Khong can cai Node.js neu goi tao bang dong-goi.bat: da kem san trong thu muc "runtime".

2. Bam dup start.bat. Cua so den hien "TikTok Live Game: http://127.0.0.1:3000" la duoc.
   start.bat tu kiem tra truoc khi chay: Node.js (thieu thi goi y cai bang winget), thu vien (thieu thi tu npm ci),
   file cau hinh dung dinh dang chua, cong 3000 co dang bi chiem khong (hoi tat), da dien tai khoan TikTok chua.
   Dong nao bao [LOI] thi lam theo huong dan ngay ben duoi dong do.
   Sau do start.bat mo quan bar kem Bang dieu khien (phim S) tren trinh duyet.
   Server chay ngay trong cua so den nay: DONG cua so = tat bridge.

3. Kiem tra: mo http://localtest.me:3000/bar.html (quan bar ban web), roi tren Bang dieu khien
   (http://127.0.0.1:3000/control.html) bam DEMO 30 hoac DEMO 200 -> nguoi ao vao san.
   Xem thu khong can bridge: http://localtest.me:3000/bar.html?demo=30
   Xong thi DONG tab nay lai (de khong bi phat nhac 2 lan khi live).

4. Ket noi live: mo TikTokBridge\.env
   - TIKTOK_USERNAME=ten_tai_khoan   (khong co dau @) -> lan sau bam start.bat la tu ket noi.
     Hoac chay tam: start.bat ten_tai_khoan
   - LIVE_PROVIDER=tiktok     : noi thang TikTok (mac dinh).
     LIVE_PROVIDER=tikfinity  : di qua TikFinity Desktop (phai mo TikFinity truoc).
   Luu lai roi tat cua so den va bam start.bat lai.

5. Dua vao TikTok LIVE Studio (giong tu-tien):
   Add source -> Link -> dan:  http://localtest.me:3000/bar.html
   Bat Custom resolution 1080 x 1920, bat Turn on sound -> Add -> chon Fill screen.
   Tuy chon them vao cuoi link: ?chroma=1 (nen xanh), ?hud=0 (an bang), ?music=0 (tat nhac), ?npc=8 (so nguoi ao), ?bloom=0 (tat phat sang neu may yeu), ?sfx=0 (tat am thanh hieu ung)
   Phim: F1 an/hien bang chu, F2 nen xanh, S (hoac F3 / C) an/hien Bang dieu khien ngay tren trang.
   start.bat tu mo http://127.0.0.1:3000/bar.html?panel=1 = quan bar kem Bang dieu khien (ket noi TikTok, gia lap
   nguoi xem / tang qua, demo, bat tat nhac...). Tab nay tu tat nhac cho khoi keu 2 lan voi LIVE Studio.
   Nhac trong DJ_MUSIC thi nhan vat / camera / den giat theo nhip that cua bai (tu do BPM).
   Nhac phat tu LIVE Studio / Spotify / app khac: mo Chrome vao http://127.0.0.1:3000/beat.html (Trang nghe nhip),
   bam "Nghe am thanh may tinh" -> chon Toan bo man hinh -> tick "Chia se ca am thanh he thong" -> Chia se.
   Trang chi nghe de bat nhip, khong phat lai am thanh. De cua so do mo trong luc live (thu nho cung duoc).
   Nhac nen: tha MP3 vao DJ_MUSIC (hoac Build\DJ_MUSIC). Man LED: tha video MP4 / anh PNG vao DJ_VIDEO.
   Ban Unity cu: dat GAME_MODE=unity trong TikTokBridge\.env, start.bat se mo Build\TIKTOK_LIVE_BAR.exe,
   dua vao LIVE Studio bang Game Capture.

6. Chuyen sang may khac: bam dong-goi.bat -> file zip trong thu muc "dong-goi" canh thu muc game.

Luu y
- run.bat cu van dung duoc (goi sang start.bat).
- Ban Unity dung san chi ket noi cong 3000: giu PORT=3000 trong TikTokBridge\.env (ban web thi cong nao cung duoc).
- TikTokBridge\.env co EULER_API_KEY (neu ban dien): dung gui file zip cho nguoi la.
